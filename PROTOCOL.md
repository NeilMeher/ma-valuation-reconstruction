# Research protocol

Frozen 2026-11-18 for the TaskUs pilot before reconstruction. The comparison is a disclosure audit with two paired forecast cases for one deal. There is no fitted model or train/test split. The published offer and adviser values are external checks. The primary metric is reconstructed minus published low and high endpoint, in USD per share. Report the maximum absolute endpoint error per case. Compare with the nearest-dime disclosure interval (±$0.05); rounded inputs can cause further differences. Do not tune parameters to the reported values.

Baseline uses the disclosed Dec 31 2024 valuation date, 2025–29 stock-compensation-burdened unlevered FCF, normalized terminal FCF, and the exhibit's discount factors (midyear for forecast cash flows and perpetuity; year-end terminal for multiple). The terminal growth convention is to grow the normalized base FCF one year. Use case-specific diluted shares as reported. Sensitivities change one convention at a time: 2029 FCF as terminal base, end-year discounting, hypothetical 2025 stub, March 2025 net debt and the alternate disclosed diluted share count. These are scenarios, with no claim that they bound an unknown adviser result.

Expansion rule, fixed ahead of screening: U.S.-domiciled public targets in IT consulting, digital transformation or outsourced customer/business services, all-cash offers for the unaffiliated common equity announced 2021–2025, selecting one financial adviser and its primary standalone DCF per deal. Search SEC DEFM14A, S-4 and 13E-3. Record incomplete observations even where forecasts or bridge inputs are absent. Exclude foreign and private targets and mixed consideration. Target 8–12 observations. Avoid extrapolating a convenience sample to a population.
