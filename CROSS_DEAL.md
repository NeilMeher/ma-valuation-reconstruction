# Cross-deal reproducibility

Five were incomplete in the reviewed disclosures and three other candidates were excluded. See the [screening log](screening.csv) and [expansion protocol](EXPANSION.md). Prices are USD per share. Terminal enterprise values marked “terminal EV” are USD millions, before forecast cash flows or capitalization adjustments.

### Source and inference ledger

All documents were read on 2026-11-18. Values were transcribed as disclosed. The generated [cross-deal results](cross_deal.json) use [calculation inputs](cross_inputs.json) and independent [benchmarks](cross_checks.csv). Offer prices serve only as context for the disclosed ranges. Forecasts and capitalization figures are in USD millions, share counts are in millions, rates are decimals, and time is measured in years. Filings include adviser and other third-party materials. This repository distributes transcribed facts and derived values. The filing images and complete originals remain at the linked sources.

- **TaskUs.** Accession 0001193125-25-153528, Exhibit (c)(ix), slides 18, 20 and 23. See the [pilot input ledger](SOURCES.md) for dates, separate forecast cases and timing checks.
- **Startek.** Accession 0001140361-23-051449, exhibit (c)(3), October 10, 2023, slides 11 and 15. Standalone forecast unlevered cash flows were $4.1m for H2 2023 and $17.1m, $18.7m, $20.0m, $21.7m and $23.3m for 2024–28. Normalized terminal FCF was $20.3m. Bridge was $34.9m cash plus $1.1m Argentina net value less $78.9m debt, all as of June 30, and the slide 11 dilution estimate was 40.4m. October 10 to December 31 gives an inferred first midpoint of 41/365 years. Full-year forecast midpoints are shifted by 102/365 years relative to June 30. Baseline terminal discounting uses the final forecast midpoint. The alternate terminal date adds half a year. Leaving the forecasts on the unshifted June 30 schedule, with terminal timing held fixed, moves the endpoints to $3.13–$4.80. [October fairness deck](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1031029/000114036123051449/ny20012221x3_exc-3.htm)
- **Sykes.** Accession 0001193125-21-223651, proxy pp. 49–50 and 53, using the stated adviser forecast and FY2025 EBITDA of $295m as terminal base. Accession 0001564590-21-045176, August supplement, gives $65m net cash as of March 31, 2021 and about 41m fully diluted shares as of June 15. Time from the March 31 valuation anchor to year-end 2025 is 4.75 years. Spot dates for the nine-month stub and later forecasts use midperiod by assumption. Putting the terminal discount at the final forecast midpoint increases the discrepancy. Timing and the rounded share count are reasons for caution, and the endpoint spread remains unresolved.
