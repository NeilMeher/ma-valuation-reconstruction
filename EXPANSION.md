# Cross-deal disclosure audit protocol

Frozen before calculating additional DCFs. Select one final standalone adviser range per eligible acquisition in the original protocol. Government IT consulting and cash tender offers qualify if they meet the same target, year, and consideration criteria. The sample is purposive. The SEC filing and an available relevant amendment or exhibit are the unit of document review. Record the named missing input if the reviewed material is insufficient. No model is fitted and there is no statistical split.

Apply the pilot metric, signed low and high errors in U.S. dollars per share, only if both corners can be computed using separately disclosed forecast cash flows, terminal inputs, a capitalization bridge, and share count. Where an exhibit supplies rounded discount conventions, use the midpoint of each forecast period, then value at the specified anchor. When the anchor comes after the first period's midpoint and the exhibit uses a current valuation date, treat the first period as a remaining-year stub with the disclosed forecast cash flow and discount by half the remaining calendar time. Keep one counterfactual with unshifted forecast period midpoints, labeled as a timing sensitivity. For a growth DCF, use normalized terminal cash flow grown once, discounted at the final forecast midpoint by default, and show year-end terminal timing separately if uncertain. Read contemporaneous points and dates from the filing, and keep offer and endpoints outside reconstruction inputs. Use disclosed rounded diluted counts when available. Never infer a missing input from published equity or valuation range. Partial component checks do not count as complete reconstructions.
